Create a SharePoint list named "Patient Tracker" with the following columns: in site https://jpower4mvp.sharepoint.com.mcas.ms/sites/CodeApp

1. Title (Single Line of Text) - Patient ID (Example: PAT-0001)
2. Patient Name (Single Line of Text)
3. Gender (Choice: Male, Female, Other)
4. Date of Birth (Date Only)
5. Mobile Number (Single Line of Text)
6. Email (Single Line of Text)
7. Address (Multiple Lines of Text)
8. Blood Group (Choice: A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-)
9. Department (Single Line of Text)
10. Doctor Assigned (Person or Group)
11. Visit Type (Choice: New, Follow-up, Emergency, Consultation)
12. Date (Date Only)
13. Discharge Date (Date Only)
14. Notes (Multiple Lines of Text)
